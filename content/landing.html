Does Cova know my professor’s exam dates?

You add your section’s dates to your calendar. Course review does not guarantee a specific instructor’s coverage or exam format. Always check your syllabus.

Can I use Cova without a Canvas import?

Yes. Available Cova practice works without importing Canvas files. Imported materials, when available, add source references within your own account.

What does Cova record?

Your account saves course selections and study progress. Cova also measures site visits and study actions to improve the product. Analytics events exclude names, emails, written answers, and Canvas file content. Private course materials stay in your account.

Does one correct answer mean I have mastered a topic?

No. Practice progress helps you choose what to study next. A single correct answer is not a mastery claim or a prediction of your course grade.
